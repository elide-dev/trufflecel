# Contributor agreement

TruffleCEL accepts first-party contributions under **Apache-2.0 OR MIT**, at the
recipient's option. By intentionally submitting work for inclusion, you offer
it under both license alternatives in [LICENSE](LICENSE), including the applicable
Apache-2.0 patent terms. You retain your copyright. There is no copyright assignment
or separate signed contributor license agreement.

Submit only work you are authorized to contribute under these terms, including
any necessary employer authorization. Identify externally sourced code, data,
fonts, schemas and patches in the pull request. Preserve their copyright,
license texts and provenance. The first-party grant does not relicense dependencies
or vendored materials; see [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md) and [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Assisted contributions and attribution

The same requirements apply to human, bot and AI-assisted work. The submitter
remains responsible for understanding the change, checking its provenance,
running appropriate validation and resolving review findings. Describe material
tool assistance or generated artifacts when it helps reviewers assess the change.
Never invent authorship, approvals, test results or a human certification.

TruffleCEL does not currently require DCO sign-offs. If you voluntarily add a
`Signed-off-by` trailer, it must reflect your own actual certification; do not
copy another contributor's trailer. Sign-offs and cryptographic Git signatures
are different mechanisms. Neither substitutes for independent review.

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), and the
[code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Sensitive reports use the private
[security process](SECURITY.md).
