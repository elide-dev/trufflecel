# Code of conduct

TruffleCEL welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed community spaces, as well as interactions while representing
the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss technical ideas on their merits, explain
disagreements, accept feedback, and make room for newcomers. Respect people's
privacy and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another
person's private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern to a repository maintainer through a private contact method
listed on their GitHub profile. If a maintainer is involved, contact another
maintainer or an [Elide organization](https://github.com/elide-dev) owner.
Avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Maintainers should handle reports
with discretion and share details only as needed to investigate and respond.

Maintainers may request changes, remove content, close or lock discussions, or
restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on the severity
and pattern of behavior. They should explain decisions to affected participants
when doing so is safe. Participants may ask an uninvolved maintainer or
organization owner to review an enforcement decision.
